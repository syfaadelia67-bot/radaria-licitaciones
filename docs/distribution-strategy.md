# TenderSignal zero-cost distribution strategy

This document is updated only when the operating strategy changes. The live market brief and reusable posts are generated automatically from verified TED data.

## Objective

Acquire qualified suppliers without paid advertising or unsolicited mass outreach.

## Distribution loop

1. TED notices are fetched and validated.
2. TenderSignal generates individual opportunity pages.
3. A public market brief summarizes the latest verified inventory.
4. Reusable, fact-based posts point to the brief and relevant opportunity pages.
5. Interested suppliers can request a ranked fit report through the attributed Tally application.

## Publishing rules

- Never claim traffic, customers, revenue, endorsements or scarcity that do not exist.
- Never copy full official notices; link to the authoritative TED record.
- Publish only to communities where public-procurement opportunities are relevant and self-promotion is permitted.
- Adapt the generated copy to each community instead of cross-posting identical spam.
- Record distribution outcomes without storing personal application data in GitHub.

## Initial channels

- The TenderSignal public website and RSS feed.
- The repository README and GitHub discovery surfaces.
- Relevant founder, small-agency, software-supplier and procurement communities that allow useful resource posts.
- Search-engine discovery through sitemap submission after owner authorization.

## Success signals

- brief visits;
- opportunity-page visits;
- attributed founder-application submissions;
- qualified non-owner applications;
- explicit willingness-to-pay responses.
