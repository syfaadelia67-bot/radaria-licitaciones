Professional supervision and occupational safety coordinator - Construction and reconstruction works of OŠ Vodice

The subject of procurement is the services of professional supervision and occupational safety coordinator during the construction and reconstruction works of OŠ Vodice as part of the project "Reconstruction and extension of OŠ Vodice, NPOO.C3.1.R1-I2.01-V1.0071," in accordance with the cost estimates which are an integral part of this Procurement Documentation. The supervision services under this contract include professional supervision services in accordance with the Construction Act (NN 155/25), the Act on Spatial Planning and Construction Activities (NN 78/15, 118/18, 110/19), and other applicable regulations. It will be considered that the economic operator has thoroughly studied this Procurement Documentation and its annexes before submitting the offer, has obtained all necessary data affecting the provision of the subject services at the specified locations, and has submitted their offer based on all of this.

Stručni nadzor i koordinator zaštite na radu- Radovi izgradnje i rekonstrukcije OŠ Vodice

Predmet nabave su usluge stručnog nadzora i koordinatora zaštite na radu tijekom radova izgradnje i rekonstrukcije OŠ Vodice u sklopu projekta "Rekonstrukcija i dogradnja OŠ Vodice, NPOO.C3.1.R1-I2.01-V1.0071, sukladno troškovnicima koji čine sastavni dio ove Dokumentacije o nabavi. Usluge nadzora u okviru ovog ugovora obuhvaćaju usluge stručnog nadzora u smislu Zakona o gradnji (NN 155/25), Zakona o poslovima i djelatnostima prostornog uređenja i gradnje (NN 78/15, 118/18, 110/19) te drugih važećih propisa. Smatrat će se da je gospodarski subjekt prije podnošenja ponude u svemu proučio ovu Dokumentaciju o nabavi i njezine priloge, da je došao do svih potrebnih podataka koji utječu na pružanje predmetnih usluga na navedenim lokacijama, te da je na osnovu svega toga podnio svoju ponudu.
